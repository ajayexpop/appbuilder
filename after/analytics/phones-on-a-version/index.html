How to see which phones run one app version

On the Devices tab, Show devices narrows the list of phones to one app version and platform.

Steps

  1. In Analytics, open the Devices tab.
  2. Under “Versions in use”, find the version in the table under the Versions chart. Each row gives the version and build, the platform, the installs and when it was last seen.
  3. Press Show devices on that row. The list below is narrowed to that version and platform, and the page scrolls to it.
  4. To see every phone again, press Clear.

What happens next

  • The versions are those phones reported over the last 30 days; each install counts once, under the version it reported last.
  • The list shows, for each phone, the person signed in (name and email, or “Not signed in”), the phone and its system, the app version, a city and country located from the phone's network, and when it was last seen.
  • Show devices sets the list's App version and Platform filters. Clear resets every filter of the list and the search; it shows only when one differs from its default.

If something goes wrong

No version has reported yet
Instead of the chart, with “Versions appear here once people open a version of the app built here.”
No phone with the app reported in the last 30 days.
Build the app and open it on a phone: see Build & test.
No device matches
Instead of the list, with “Try other filters, or clear them.”
No phone fits the filters.
Press Clear.
The devices could not be loaded.
Instead of the list.
The list could not be read.
Press Try again.
