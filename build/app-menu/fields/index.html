ButtonWhat it does
EventEvent and language barReloads the section on the chosen event. Unsaved changes are dropped without asking.

Move downThe four menu sections: Event (everyone), My account (participants), My company (team members), My company (exhibitor)Moves the item one place down within its section, in the draft. Disabled on the last item (“Already last in this section”).
EditThe four menu sections: Event (everyone), My account (participants), My company (team members), My company (exhibitor)Opens the item's window, headed with the item's name.
Remove from the menuThe four menu sections: Event (everyone), My account (participants), My company (team members), My company (exhibitor)Takes the item out of the draft at once, without asking. After “Save changes” it is gone from this event's menu in every language, with its content.
AddThe four menu sections: Event (everyone), My account (participants), My company (team members), My company (exhibitor)Adds the chosen feature or page at the end of the section, in the draft: with its own name and icon, not hidden, visible to everyone, not in the bottom bar
Fields and switches of the sections and the item editorThe four menu sections: Event (everyone), My account (participants), My company (team members), My company (exhibitor)The sections' own fields and switches change the unsaved draft and the preview only; nothing is sent until “Save changes”. The item editor's fields change only the editor until its “Save”, which saves the whole menu at once.
CloseThe item's windowCloses the editor without keeping its changes (the × at the top right)
ChooseThe item's windowOpens “Choose an icon”
CancelThe item's windowCloses the editor without keeping its changes. Clicking outside the dialog does the same. After a “Save” that was refused or failed, the change is already in the page's unsaved menu, so closing does not undo it there.
SaveThe item's windowSaves the whole menu of this event at once, for the language picked at the top: the editor's changes and any other unsaved change on the page (changed section headers first, then the menu). On success App Studio says “Saved” and the editor closes. When the save is refused or fails, the editor stays open with the message, and the change stays in the page's unsaved menu. Disabled while the name is empty (except for a “Your page” item) and while a save runs.
