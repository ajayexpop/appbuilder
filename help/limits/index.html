Custom period
Filters
A period is at most 92 days, from and to included. On Apply the two days are put in order, the end is cut to today and, if the range is wider than 92 days, its end is kept and its start moved up. Cancel or closing the calendar drops the choice. The calendar is shown in English (en-US) dates.
