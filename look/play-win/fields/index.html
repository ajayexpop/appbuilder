Buttons and controls

ButtonWhat it does
Discard changesPlay & WinThrows away this event's unsaved edits; what was saved stays as it was.
UploadPicture and linkOpens the file chooser and then the crop window; nothing is sent yet. Reads Replace once a picture is set.
UploadPicture and linkThe button of the crop window: uploads the cropped picture and stores it for this app at once, before Save Play & Win.
Or paste a link to a picturePicture and linkA link box: leaving it (or pressing Enter) with a link in it makes App Studio fetch that picture and keep it for this app at once, before “Save Play & Win”.
Use defaultPicture and linkShown once a picture is set: goes back to the default illustration in the form; saved with Save Play & Win.
Open the picturePicture and linkThe thumbnail of a set picture: opens the stored picture in a new tab. Only reads; changes nothing.
The title bar of a text card (Voting screen, Thank-you screen and the others)TextsOpens or closes the card. The Voting screen card starts open. Changes nothing.
Use defaultTextsShown once a text is edited: puts back the app's own wording for this text in this language, in the form; saved with Save Play & Win.
BoldTextsMakes the selected words bold in the form; saved with Save Play & Win.
ItalicTextsMakes the selected words italic in the form; saved with Save Play & Win.
Accent colourTextsMarks the selected words with the accent (the dot button); saved with Save Play & Win.
Copy linkKiosk (stand iPad)Copies the kiosk link to the clipboard; the button then reads “Copied”. Nothing is saved.
OpenKiosk (stand iPad)Opens the kiosk page in a new tab. Disabled until the link is saved. Opening it changes nothing, but anyone who plays there makes a real entry.
New linkKiosk (stand iPad)Asks for confirmation, then makes a new private link in the form. The old link stops working when you save.
Make a new linkKiosk (stand iPad)Confirms the new link in the form; nothing is sent until Save Play & Win.
CancelKiosk (stand iPad)Closes the confirmation and keeps the current link.
Use defaultKiosk (stand iPad)Shown once the kiosk consent is edited: puts back the kiosk's default consent for this language in the form; saved with Save Play & Win.
RefreshEntriesReads the entries again. Only reads; changes nothing.
Try againEntriesAfter a load error: reads the entries again. Only reads; changes nothing.
Download CSVEntriesSaves a CSV file of the entries on this computer; nothing changes in App Studio. Disabled while there are no entries.
Trash button on each rowEntriesThe trash button of a row: opens the confirmation only. Its accessible name, read out by screen readers, is “Delete [name]'s entry”.
Delete entryEntriesDeletes the entry for good: it leaves the prize draw and cannot be undone. The list then reloads. The confirmation is titled “Delete this entry?”.
Keep itEntriesCloses the confirmation; the entry stays.
Save Play & WinThe save bar at the bottomSaves this event's Play & Win: switches, result, picture, link, texts and kiosk, all together. Other events stay as they are. Disabled while nothing has changed; while a field is highlighted, it shows a message and saves nothing.
HomePreviewStarts the phone preview on the app's home screen. Only the preview changes; nothing is saved.
My BadgePreviewStarts the phone preview on My Badge, signed in. Only the preview changes; nothing is saved.
Boot flowPreviewStarts the phone preview at the splash, then sign-in, then choosing the event. Only the preview changes; nothing is saved.
Signed-in previewPreviewShown while Home is chosen: shows the home screen as a signed-in attendee sees it. Only the preview changes; nothing is saved.
Guest previewPreviewShown while Home is chosen: shows the home screen as a guest sees it. Only the preview changes; nothing is saved.
