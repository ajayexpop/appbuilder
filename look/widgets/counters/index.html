How to choose what Event stats counts

Choose up to three counters for Event stats, in the order you want. Each person sees their own numbers.

Before you start

Switch Event stats on first: the counter buttons work only while it is on. Switching it on also ships the widget with your next build.

Steps

  1. Under Counters, in order, remove a counter with its bin button.
  2. Add a counter with its button: Upcoming Meetings, Upcoming Sessions, Scanned Badges, Chats or Unread Messages. These buttons show while fewer than three counters are chosen.
  3. Use the up and down arrows to set the order.
  4. Press Save changes. “Widgets saved” confirms it.

What happens next

  • Save changes saves the counters and colours of all three widgets in one go.
  • App Studio says: “What a widget shows updates on phones at their next refresh, with no new build.”
  • The preview shows sample numbers: “Sample numbers. Each person sees their own.”

If something goes wrong
