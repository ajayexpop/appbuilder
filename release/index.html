Release

Release follows App Studio's Release group: Store screenshots, Build & test and Publish. Here you make your store pictures, build the app and send it to your testers, then ask ExpoPlatform to publish it in the App Store and Google Play.

Pages in this tab

  • Store screenshots: take pictures of your app on a test device with your event and sample people, turn them into store artwork in Store studio, and attach them to your store listing.
  • Build & test: build the app for iPhone and Android, then install it on your phone or send it to your testers through TestFlight or Google Play internal testing.
  • Publish: write the store listing in each language, give the store reviewers a demo account, ask ExpoPlatform to publish the app, then follow the store's review and the rollout.
  • From test build to the stores: how a version of your app goes from your testers to the stores, through the store listing, ExpoPlatform's approval, the store's review and the rollout.
