Buttons and controls

ButtonWhat it does
Store screenshotsStore screenshotsThe sidebar entry under “Release” opens this section.
ScreenshotsStore screenshotsShows the half where you take screenshots, follow the runs and manage your pictures (the default). Both halves stay loaded, so a half-made Store studio set survives a look at the captures. Nothing is sent.
Store studioStore screenshotsShows the half that turns pictures into store artwork. It is already loaded with the page (Publish links straight to it). Nothing is sent.
EventTake new screenshotsChanges the event to capture, and loads that event's menu screens for “Screens to capture”. Nothing is saved.
StoreTake new screenshotsChooses the store for the next capture. Nothing is sent until “Take screenshots”.
RefreshTake new screenshotsLoads the captures and your screenshots again.
Take screenshotsTake new screenshotsStarts a capture at once, with no confirmation. When the app already has a screenshot build for this store, the capture uses that build. When it has none, it starts a new app build first and the capture follows on its own once the build succeeds. It sends the event, the store and the ticked screens. Disabled while no event is chosen; while a capture is in progress it shows a spinner and reads “Capturing...”, and the note beside it says the next capture can start when that one is done.
Choose screensScreens to captureShows or hides the per-account-type checklists (the button then reads “Hide”). Nothing is sent.
Visitor screensScreens to captureTicks or unticks every visitor screen at once (“Exhibitor screens” does the same for exhibitors); disabled when the account type has no screens. Nothing is sent until “Take screenshots”.
a screen's checkboxScreens to captureTicks or unticks one menu screen for the next capture. Nothing is sent until “Take screenshots”.
StopCapturesStops a capture that is still in progress, at once and with no confirmation: the message “Capture stopped” appears, and the capture shows “Failed” with the line “Cancelled by admin”. When the capture was still waiting for its app build, that build keeps running. “Stop” shows on the capture's card and on its row in the list.
page arrows under the listCapturesShows another page of runs (five per page; only when there are more than five). Nothing is sent.
StoreYour screenshotsSwitches the gallery between “Android ([n])” and “iOS ([n])”; the count is the store's screenshots, not its videos. Nothing is sent.
DeviceYour screenshotsSwitches between “Phone” and “Tablet” pictures; shown only when the store's pictures include both. Nothing is sent.
LanguageYour screenshotsShows “All languages” or one language's pictures; shown only when the pictures come in more than one language. Pictures without a language (your uploads) are hidden when one language is chosen. Nothing is sent.
a picture tile (the picture with its name under it)Your screenshotsOpens the full-size “Preview” at that picture, with “Previous”, “Next” and “Close”. Nothing is sent.
Delete [name]Your screenshotsThe trash icon on a tile (its tooltip): opens the confirmation only. Nothing is sent yet.
Keep itYour screenshotsCloses the confirmation; the picture stays.
DeleteYour screenshotsDeletes the picture from your gallery, and its file with it, then loads the gallery again. A new capture takes a captured picture again; an uploaded one is gone for good.
the account-type select beside 'Upload'Add your ownChooses the account type for the next upload. Nothing is sent.
UploadAdd your ownOpens the file picker; choosing a file uploads it at once, checked against the store's rules, into your gallery.
a clip's video playerFeature videoPlays a clip ExpoPlatform recorded. If the clip fails to load, App Studio gets a fresh link for it once.
DownloadFeature videoGets a fresh link for the clip, then your browser opens the clip's file to download it.
Try againFeature videoUnder a clip that could not load: gets a fresh link for it once more.
Choose logoThe listingOpens a file picker (“Replace logo” once a logo is set). The logo stays in this page only: nothing is sent or saved.
RemoveThe listingTakes the logo out of the set (shown only when a logo is set). Nothing is sent.
the colour, typeface and logo settings aboveThe listingChange the set held in the page; these settings are never saved. A typeface choice also loads that face from Google Fonts in the browser.
Move earlierScreensMoves the screen one place up (“Move later” one place down); the order is the order the store shows. Nothing is sent.
Remove [name]ScreensRemoves the screen from the list. The Homepage cannot be removed. Nothing is sent.
the screen's picture box ('No picture' or the picture)ScreensOpens the picker “Your screenshots”, like “Choose from your screenshots”. Nothing is sent.
Choose from your screenshotsScreensOpens the picker “Your screenshots” (“Change picture” once a picture is set). Nothing is sent.
a picture in the pickerScreensPuts that picture on the screen. App Studio reads it again from your screenshots or, for a picture the stores show, from the store listing.
UploadScreensOpens a file picker. A chosen PNG or JPEG only opens the crop dialog “Frame the phone screen” (any other type is refused with the message “Stores take only PNG or JPEG screenshots”); nothing is sent until “Use this”.
Use thisScreensIn “Frame the phone screen”: puts the framed picture (1080 x 2160) on the screen at once and uploads it to your screenshots, filed as Android, visitor, phone.
Add a screenScreensAdds a screen named “Screen [n]”, in the set, with no picture. Nothing is sent.
CloseScreensCloses the picker without choosing.
RunwayCompositionOne of the six system buttons (Runway, Stand, Spectrum, Editorial, Frame, Immersive): lays out the whole set in that system. Nothing is sent.
the other settings of this cardCompositionChange the set held in the page; these settings are never saved.
a store size checkboxStore sizesAdds or removes that size from what is written. Nothing is sent.
Show seamsPreviewShows or hides the seam lines in the preview. Nothing is sent.
the preview's size select (beside 'Show seams')PreviewChooses the store size the preview draws. Nothing is sent.
Attach to the store listingSend to the storesDraws every screen of the set at each ticked size that the listing takes and puts the files into the listing's screenshot slots for the chosen language: it uploads the new pictures first, removes that language's earlier pictures in those slots only once every new one is in (or takes the new ones back out if an upload fails), then puts them in the set's order. When the slots already hold pictures it first asks to replace them. It changes App Studio's copy of your store listing only; Publish sends the listing, pictures included, to the stores. Disabled, with the reason in a tooltip, while a store check blocks, while the listing has no language, or while a problem in the plan remains.
ReplaceSend to the storesIn “Replace the listing's screenshots?”: runs the attach (uploads, removes and reorders listing pictures for one language).
CancelSend to the storesCloses “Replace the listing's screenshots?” without changing anything.
Download zipSend to the storesDraws every screen of the set at every ticked size in the browser, packs them in a zip named after the app and downloads it; nothing is sent to App Studio. Disabled with no picture in the set, no size ticked, or a blocking store check.
Listing languageSend to the storesChooses the listing language the attach would replace and updates the plan. Nothing is sent.
Try againSend to the storesAfter the listing could not be read: loads the store listing again.
Open PublishSend to the storesOpens Publish (“See them in Publish” after an attach does the same).
