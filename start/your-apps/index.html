Your apps

Your apps is the first page App Studio shows. It lists your organisation's apps, one tile each, with what each store has, the latest test build and the event that matters now.

What you can do here

  • Open an app: click anywhere on its tile. The app opens on its Overview.
  • Start a new app with New app. Nothing is created until you press Create in the dialog. See Add a new app.
  • See where each app stands in the App Store and Google Play, and how its latest test build went.
  • Find an app in a long list. With 7 or more apps, a search field, “Find an app by name or number”, shows under the heading. Type part of an app's name, in any case, or of its app number; empty the field to see every app again.
  • Play this page's part of App Studio's Guide again with Guide. See App Studio's built-in Guide.
  • Switch App Studio between light and dark with the moon or sun button; its tooltip says Dark theme or Light theme.
  • Come back here from any page of an app: press Your apps at the top left.

The screen

How it works

  • Your apps lists your organisation's apps in App Studio. Archived apps are not listed.
  • Under the heading, a line counts your apps, such as 1 app. With no app at all, there is no count and the page says “No apps yet. Press “New app” to make your first one.”
  • Each tile shows the app's icon, its name and its app number, such as “App 231”. An app with no icon shows the first letters of the first two words of its name instead.
  • Under App Store and Google Play, the first badge is what people can download now from that store. A second badge, when there is one, is the newest release on its way to that store.
  • Latest test build is the app's newest test build on iPhone or Android: whether it is building, ready or failed, its platform and version, and how long ago it finished, started or was queued.
  • The event row shows one event, with its dates: the event on now (Happening now), else the next one to start (Next event), else the one that ended last (Last event). Events without dates are never shown there. With more than one event, the label adds the number of events, such as “Next event · 3 events”.
  • Each row of a tile is read on its own. A row that cannot be read is left out rather than guessed, so a tile may have no store rows, no build row or no event row. A missing Latest test build row can also mean the app has no test build yet.
  • While a tile loads, its name and app number show at once, with grey placeholders for the rest. Each tile fills in on its own.

What people can download now (first badge)

LiveThe store reports a version as live, and the badge names it, for example “Live · 1.94.4”. It reads “Live” alone when no version name is known.
Not live yetThe app has a record in that store, but nothing is live.
Not publishedThe app has no record in that store.
Removed from the storeThe app's record in that store is suspended or removed.

The newest release on its way (second badge)

The second badge shows the state of the app's newest store release, followed by its version, such as “In review · 1.4.0”. Releases in other states add no badge.

PreparingThe release is being made ready: a draft, uploading, processing, ready or staged.
In reviewThe release was submitted and the store is reviewing it.
ApprovedThe release passed the store's review. Your ExpoPlatform team starts the rollout; ask your ExpoPlatform contact.
Rolling outThe release is reaching people step by step.
Rollout stoppedThe rollout was halted.
RejectedThe store turned the release down.

Latest test build and event

BuildingThe newest test build is queued or building.
ReadyThe newest test build finished. The line under it reads, for example, “Android 1.106.2”, then how long ago.
FailedThe newest test build failed.
No event in the app yetThe app has no event. The row is then labelled Events.
Untitled eventThe event shown has no title.

If something goes wrong

No apps yet. Press "New app" to make your first one.
Your organisation has no app in App Studio yet.
Press New app to make your first one. See Add a new app.
No app matches
Your search matches no app's name or app number. The message ends with your search text in quotation marks.
No app on the list has that text in its name or number.
Check the text, or empty the field to see every app again.

App Studio's own guide says

Welcome to App Studio
“This guide walks you through it once - you can open it again any time with "Guide".”
Your apps
“Every app of your organisation is listed here with its store status, latest test build and its next event.”
Guide
“Open this guide again whenever you need it. Each page has its own part.”
Custom pages, Play & Win and Home-screen widgets have no part of the Guide: on those three pages, Guide does nothing.

Set by your ExpoPlatform team

Set by your ExpoPlatform team
Your ExpoPlatform team can archive an app. An archived app is no longer listed.
